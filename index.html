First Month Letter

To My Beloved Naya,

26.10.2026 • One Month with You

They say that time is merely a river measured in seconds, yet since the moment you stepped into my life, every hour has been measured in the quiet rhythm of my heart and the stillness of your smile. Thirty days might seem like an ordinary breath on a fleeting calendar, but to me, this first month has felt like stumbling upon a quiet, golden sanctuary in a hurried world. In just thirty sunrises, you have painted my existence in colors and warmth I never even knew I was longing for.

Looking back, I still vividly remember the nervous boy under the stadium lights, clutching a carton of milk with trembling hands just to catch a glimpse of your laughter. I remember walking beside you on that quiet track, praying that our footsteps would never reach an end. From the very first photograph you ever sent me to the unrehearsed silliness of our first date, every memory with you has settled so deeply into my soul. With you, Naya, I never have to search for words or wear a disguise; in your presence, I am effortlessly, peacefully at home.

Thank you for the grace with which you love, the kindness that radiates from your eyes, and the gentleness that softens even my heaviest days. Thank you for choosing to hold my hand, for allowing me into your universe, and for giving me the greatest privilege of walking this journey as your partner.

As this melody quietly whispers between us—no matter what the horizon holds, no matter what storms may brew or how cold the nights may become, remember this solemn promise: I will never let go of your hand. Through every quiet dawn, every bustling afternoon, and every winding crossroad of this life, kita lewati berdua. We shall weather, endure, and cherish every single season together.

Happy 1st Month Anniversary, Mahira Fauzia Abhinaya.

May this only be the gentle opening verse of an endless, beautiful story we shall write together for a lifetime.

With all the devotion and tenderness my heart will ever know,

— Paw
